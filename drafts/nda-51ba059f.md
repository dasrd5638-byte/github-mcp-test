## NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective as of [CURRENT DATE],

**BY AND BETWEEN:**

**GOOGLE LLC**, a [STATE/COUNTRY OF INCORPORATION] company, with its principal place of business at [GOOGLE'S ADDRESS] (hereinafter "Disclosing Party");

**AND**

**ABHAY KANT MISHRA**, residing at [ABHAY'S ADDRESS] (hereinafter "Receiving Party").

**WHEREAS**, the Receiving Party is joining the Disclosing Party as a Software Developer and, in connection therewith, may be exposed to and receive certain confidential, proprietary, and trade secret information belonging to the Disclosing Party; and

**WHEREAS**, the parties wish to establish the terms and conditions governing the disclosure and protection of such confidential information.

**NOW, THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises herein contained, the parties agree as follows:

1.  **Definition of Confidential Information**. "Confidential Information" shall mean any and all information, whether written or oral, tangible or intangible, disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, directly or indirectly, including but not limited to, technical information, business information, financial information, salary information, proprietary data, trade secrets, know-how, designs, specifications, algorithms, software code, research, product plans, products, services, customers, markets, developments, inventions, processes, strategies, forecasts, and personnel information. Confidential Information also includes any information that the Disclosing Party has a duty to protect from unauthorized disclosure.

2.  **Obligations of Receiving Party**. The Receiving Party agrees to:
    a.  Maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence.
    b.  Not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party.
    c.  Use the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing duties as a Software Developer for the Disclosing Party.
    d.  Take all reasonable measures to protect the secrecy of and avoid disclosure or unauthorized use of the Confidential Information.

3.  **Exclusions from Confidential Information**. The obligations under Section 2 shall not apply to information that:
    a.  Is or becomes publicly known through no wrongful act of the Receiving Party.
    b.  Was rightfully known by the Receiving Party prior to receiving it from the Disclosing Party, without breach of any confidentiality obligation.
    c.  Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.
    d.  Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.
    e.  Is required to be disclosed by law or by an order of a court or governmental agency, provided that the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to the Disclosing Party of such requirement prior to disclosure and cooperates with the Disclosing Party in any effort to oppose or limit the disclosure.

4.  **Term of Confidentiality**. The obligations of confidentiality and non-disclosure set forth in this Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date of this Agreement.

5.  **Governing Law / Jurisdiction**. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [GOVERNING LAW / JURISDICTION], without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

6.  **Entire Agreement**. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior discussions, agreements, and understandings, whether written or oral.
